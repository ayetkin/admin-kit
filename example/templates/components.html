Confirm before acting

data-ak-confirm="Are you sure?" on any clickable element asks in a dialog first. data-ak-confirm-ok labels its button (by default the element's own text or tooltip), data-ak-confirm-tone="primary" marks an action that is not destructive. From script: adminkit.confirm(message, {ok, tone}).then(yes => …).

Busy button

adminkit.busy(button, promise) holds the button with a spinner until the promise settles, and passes its outcome on. adminkit.idle(button) releases one early.

Icons & tooltips

{{`{{icon "name"}}`}} draws inline SVG, sharp at every size; "name-filled" picks the filled set. Size it with --tblr-icon-size or Tabler's .icon-sm, .icon-md, .icon-lg.

Theme

Ghost buttons tint on hover in dark mode rather than fill. In light mode, green, yellow and orange text are darker steps of the same hues, so they read on white.
