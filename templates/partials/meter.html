{{/* Usage meter: how much of a budget or quota is gone. Amber past 75%, red once reached, so a table scans at a glance. {{template "meter" dict "used" .Spent "limit" .Limit}} An absent or non-positive limit renders nothing: there is no fill to show. Both values may be any numeric type. */}} {{define "meter"}} {{- $pct := pctOf .used .limit -}} {{- if ge $pct 0.0 -}} {{- end -}} {{end}}
